- 버튼 하나로 오늘의 순간들이 일기가 됩니다

- 회원가입 없음, 서버 없음

- 위치는 장소 이름 표시에만 사용됩니다

## 영어 (en-US)

### Name
```
Dailogger - One-Clip Diary
```

### Subtitle
```
Video moments, AI daily diary
```

### Promotional Text
```
Capture your day as short video moments. When the day ends, on-device AI writes your diary and turns it into a shareable timeline reel. Everything stays on your iPhone.
```

### Description
```
Dailogger is a video diary that captures your day one short clip at a time.

Tap record, add a one-line caption, done — time and place are tagged automatically. When the day ends, your scattered moments become a diary.

◆ Capture moments
- Segment recording, zero pressure
- Automatic time & place tags
- A cute, hand-drawn design

◆ AI daily blog
- One tap turns today's moments into a diary entry
- Apple Intelligence on-device AI — your diary never leaves your phone
- Write or edit it yourself anytime

◆ Timeline reels
- Merge the day's clips into one video
- Timeline-card style or full-size style
- Time, place, and captions baked in beautifully
- Perfect vertical video for Instagram stories

◆ A growing archive
- Replay your day like a story
- Days pile up in the calendar and archive
- Recording streak with a home-screen widget
- A gentle 9 PM reminder to capture today

◆ Privacy first
- No sign-up, no servers
- Videos, diaries, and locations stay on your iPhone
- Location is used only to name the places you were

One day, one clip at a time.
```
